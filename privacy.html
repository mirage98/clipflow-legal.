ClipFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Information We Process

ClipFlow may process videos uploaded by users, video frames, generated captions, hooks, technical information, and information necessary to operate the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to analyze videos, generate content suggestions, edit videos, manage queues, and support publishing workflows.

3. Third-Party Services

ClipFlow may interact with third-party services such as AI providers and TikTok. Information shared with these services may be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

4. Storage

Information may be stored locally or on infrastructure used to operate ClipFlow. Users should avoid uploading information they do not have permission to share.

5. Data Sharing

ClipFlow does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide requested functionality.

6. Security

Reasonable measures may be taken to protect stored information. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
